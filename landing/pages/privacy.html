Privacy

What we collect

The only personal data collected on this site is the email address you enter in the free-card form. We do not collect names, addresses, payment data, or browsing histories. Purchases on Etsy or Gumroad are handled entirely by those platforms under their own privacy policies; we never see your card details.

Why we collect it

To send you the free one-page emergency contact card you asked for, and — only if you stay subscribed — updates about new PaperLife kits. Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe.

What we do with it

One thing: deliver the PDF and (optionally) future product updates. We do not sell, rent, or trade the list, and we do not share it with advertisers.

What you fill into the PDFs

Everything you type into the generators (names, amounts, health details) is processed locally, on your own computer. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or seen by us. The PDFs you generate are yours.
